### Assignment 1 - Video Edit 

    • How did you approach the relationship between the sonic and visual components of your edit? 

My approach on the first assignment was to have the visual components of my video and the sonic component somewhat have a connection/feel, to give it a more nostalgic feel to the video. The main idea of the video was to incorporate a little bit of everything in my life, like how sometimes it feels a bit empty and lonely and also show the brighter things in my life like my friends, playing games, going out to events and exciting things. That’s where the idea of a sad and lonely acoustic music for the first part of the video first came to my head, and i also thought of a like nostalgic and kind of uplifting music for the second part. The visual components of the video was mostly shot in my apartment and i didn’t fully plan out the shots that i wanted, and that’s also the reason why sometimes some of the scenes feel way too long. The sonic component, i feel i had a good idea on how about i wanted to do it but its just now that i have reflected on it, it needs a bit more depth like maybe some ambient noises to give it some context to the video too.

    • How did you decide on the footage that you used to shape your project?

I decided based on the concept of the video, i would use multiple different places and lighting to kind of show different things. For example, like for the first part of the video, i mainly filmed the shots at night or in dark lit places to give it a somewhat lonely and secluded feel to the video. Looking back on it, i should have explained the concept better in my blog and shot a lot more scenes to further encapsulate the feeling of loneliness. And for the second part, i used some old footage of me and my friends hanging out back in my home country and some new clips i filmed here to show that i’m kinda showing that i’m starting a new chapter of my life here. The main thing that i would probably change for the video was adding more footage to add in the video, because i felt that looking back i could’ve asked for an extension to film more footage and that would make the video more interesting.

    • What did you learn about the power of sound to shape emotional experience, and how did you apply this knowledge to your project? 

In this project, i learned that the power of sound is really powerful and can really bring out emotion to a scene/clip. That’s why i mainly decided to use background music to support the emotions that i was trying to convey in my video. The sound in my project looking at it now, in my opinion is a bit bland and too basic, it need more sounds to support the music, like ambient noise and more sfx.

    • How does editing relate to the concepts of pacing and rhythm, and how did you use these concepts to shape your use of time in the project?

The pacing of the project mainly relied on the background music. I would use the main changes in the music to serve as like a point to transition into a different scene. Looking back on it now, i should’ve played around with the stop sign sound and try to mess around with the rhythm of that sound a bit more as it was shown at the beginning and at the end of the video. By playing around with it means that i could’ve matched the cuts and transitions with the rhythm of the stop sign.

    • Which parts of your personality, interests, past, and potential future did you choose to depict in your portrait? 

For this project, i took a little bit of everything for my portrait. Like how i showed my emotion, love for playing games, interests, past live and current life too. I probably should’ve stuck to one main part of myself rather than take everything all at once, because it’s a bit harder to show all of that in a 2 minute video. It’s also harder to choose the footage to show everything for the project too. 
 
    • What were the primary aesthetic choices that you made to express those ideas in your work?

The primary aesthetic choices were pretty much a dark like gritty environment for the first part of the video and a nostalgic and uplifting feel for the second part. I have no regrets using those two aesthetics, because i think those to aesthetics really suit my tone and self portrait.
