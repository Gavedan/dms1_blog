## Assignment 1 - Video Edit 

    • How did you approach the relationship between the sonic and visual components of your edit? 

My approach on the first assignment was to have the visual components of my video and the sonic component somewhat have a connection/feel, to give it a more nostalgic feel to the video. The main idea of the video was to incorporate a little bit of everything in my life, like how sometimes it feels a bit empty and lonely and also show the brighter things in my life like my friends, playing games, going out to events and exciting things. That’s where the idea of a sad and lonely acoustic music for the first part of the video first came to my head, and i also thought of a like nostalgic and kind of uplifting music for the second part. The visual components of the video was mostly shot in my apartment and i didn’t fully plan out the shots that i wanted, and that’s also the reason why sometimes some of the scenes feel way too long. The sonic component, i feel i had a good idea on how about i wanted to do it but its just now that i have reflected on it, it needs a bit more depth like maybe some ambient noises to give it some context to the video too.

    • How did you decide on the footage that you used to shape your project?

I decided based on the concept of the video, i would use multiple different places and lighting to kind of show different things. For example, like for the first part of the video, i mainly filmed the shots at night or in dark lit places to give it a somewhat lonely and secluded feel to the video. Looking back on it, i should have explained the concept better in my blog and shot a lot more scenes to further encapsulate the feeling of loneliness. And for the second part, i used some old footage of me and my friends hanging out back in my home country and some new clips i filmed here to show that i’m kinda showing that i’m starting a new chapter of my life here. The main thing that i would probably change for the video was adding more footage to add in the video, because i felt that looking back i could’ve asked for an extension to film more footage and that would make the video more interesting.

    • What did you learn about the power of sound to shape emotional experience, and how did you apply this knowledge to your project? 

In this project, i learned that the power of sound is really powerful and can really bring out emotion to a scene/clip. That’s why i mainly decided to use background music to support the emotions that i was trying to convey in my video. The sound in my project looking at it now, in my opinion is a bit bland and too basic, it need more sounds to support the music, like ambient noise and more sfx.

    • How does editing relate to the concepts of pacing and rhythm, and how did you use these concepts to shape your use of time in the project?

The pacing of the project mainly relied on the background music. I would use the main changes in the music to serve as like a point to transition into a different scene. Looking back on it now, i should’ve played around with the stop sign sound and try to mess around with the rhythm of that sound a bit more as it was shown at the beginning and at the end of the video. By playing around with it means that i could’ve matched the cuts and transitions with the rhythm of the stop sign.

    • Which parts of your personality, interests, past, and potential future did you choose to depict in your portrait? 

For this project, i took a little bit of everything for my portrait. Like how i showed my emotion, love for playing games, interests, past live and current life too. I probably should’ve stuck to one main part of myself rather than take everything all at once, because it’s a bit harder to show all of that in a 2 minute video. It’s also harder to choose the footage to show everything for the project too. 
 
    • What were the primary aesthetic choices that you made to express those ideas in your work?

The primary aesthetic choices were pretty much a dark like gritty environment for the first part of the video and a nostalgic and uplifting feel for the second part. I have no regrets using those two aesthetics, because i think those to aesthetics really suit my tone and self portrait.

## Assignment 2 - Graphic Icons 

    • How would you summarise your idea behind the suite of icons, and why did you choose the theme that you did? 

My idea for the suite of icons was the star system, mainly the zodiac signs. I chose the theme because i thought that it would be a really interesting thing to make into a suite of icons for the assignment and i mainly really liked the idea of designing the icons of it. Looking back on the idea, i think i should’ve delve deeper and did some more research to the theme because i think the end product turned out a bit half-cooked because some of the ideas really didn’t represent the zodiac signs that well. 

    • Did you iterate through a series of approaches before settling on one? What changed across those iterations, and why did you choose the one that you ended up with? 

I was mainly focused on one iteration of the series of icons, and i only changed the order of the icons. I chose the one i ended up with was because i didn’t really get the zodiac/star system thing and i just basically chose the most interesting ones to design. I realised that now, i should’ve done more research to give it more thought in making the icons and i should’ve also done multiple different iterations of the icons before settling on just one from the start.

    • How did you translate your thumbnail sketches into the final flat designs in Illustrator? Was anything lost in that translation, or gained? 

I traced my thumbnail sketches in illustrator and i thought it turned out fine, nothing was lost in translation but i should’ve refined the thumbnail sketches even more to give it more depth and for it to further reflect the theme.

    • How did you use colour to create a sense of unity and visual interest in the icons? Why did you choose the palette that you did?

The use of colour in my icons was mainly using a basic colour palette. I used a basic colour palette because i wanted it to look simple and not too over the top. But looking back on the project, i realized some of the colours don't match very well with each other. It just sort of looks off putting when i see it now, because some of the colours are light and fun, and some are too heavy and dark. If i could change anything now, i would’ve changed the colour palette to match the theme better.

 • How did the elements and principles of sound that you used in your audio design correspond to the elements and principles of design that you used in your visual design? 

I made the sound of the icons somewhat identical to what it would sound like if the zodiac signs were a real life object/thing. So for example for the Leo sign, i would use a lion sound and etc. This decision was probably the best thing i did in this project in my opinion, i think it really fit the theme and the icons really well. I could think of some changes to the sound in some icons but overall i really liked how the sounds that i put for the icons turned out

    • How did you translate the prompt that you chose from an abstract idea to a set of concrete visual designs?

I just thought of the first thing that came to mind when i saw the prompt system and that was star systems. I didn’t really put much thought into why i chose the prompt system, i just thought that it would be cool to make a star system icons based on the zodiac signs. 

## Assignment 3 - Virtual Environment 

    • How important was a working prototype to your design process? What did it offer that process?

It was really important having a working prototype, because it allowed me to experiment on different things that i want to do and i could also envision what i wanted to add to my environment later on because of it. I believe that if i didn’t experiment on some things on the prototype, like the code and special effects, i wouldn’t be able to make my environment at all.

    • How did your experience of designing and producing a three-dimensional environment differ from your experience of designing and producing a project in two-dimensional media? 

At first, it was really disorienting because it’s something new and i had never tried to design something on a three dimensional media before. There were a lot of complications, like not being able to navigate through the UI and not knowing how some of the things work and etc. But over time, i got really fond of trying out different things in Unity and from my experience it was really fun too. Making something in 3D is really different from 2D, you actually need to be somewhat more thorough in making things in 3D because if not the person who’s gonna try and play your game will notice a lot of things that are wrong in your environment.

    • What was the role of the virtual camera in your project, and how was it similar to and different from the role of the camera in Assignment 1? 

The role of the virtual camera in my project was to play the role of myself going through the change i was going through in my life. It’s similar to the role of the camera in Assignment one but in a different way, what i mean by that is that they both explore the changes in my life but one is showing it by showing how it changed in reality while the other showed it by showing a sort of metaphor/symbolic environment of my headspace.

    • How did you use sound in your project, and how was this similar to and different from your use of sound in Assignments 1 and 2? 

I mainly used background music and ambient noises in my project. I used a similar idea from Assignment 1, and that is using two different music for two different zones/things. I used the same idea because i felt like it really encapsulates the feeling of nostalgia and growth really well and i elevated in from Assignment 1 by adding more ambient noise/sounds to give it more depth to the environment.

    • How did you unify the different elements of your virtual environment under a certain theme or concept? How did your theme or concept evolve over the project’s development? 

I unified all the elements that i took by using elements that fit the theme of my environment. By that i mean, i used things that represent my growth and past and future to fit in my environment. The theme progressively evolved through the development of my project, like i added certain things to make the environment less bland and have more things to look at so it’s not that empty.

    • What did you discover through the process of play testing that you wouldn’t have discovered otherwise?

I discovered that even though i thought that the environment was basically ready, it was still a rough prototype. There were still things that i needed to add to make it seem more realistic and not too bland and empty. Overall if i didn’t do any playtesting, i wouldn’t have notice the subtle things needed to further elevate my environment.
